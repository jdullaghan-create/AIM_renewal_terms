AIM: Renewal Terms

Tracking whether Renewal Opportunities are converting client contract terms from single-year (<24 mo) to multi-year (≥24 mo) commitments, or the reverse — comparing each opportunity's originating contract term to its currently quoted or booked term. Renewal Opportunities in Salesforce are created 12 months before contract expiration.

Methodology & data notes

  • Population: Opportunities with Record Type Renewals and Close Date between Aug 1, 2026 and Jul 31, 2027 (current fiscal quarter through three quarters out; FY runs Feb 1–Jan 31). Closed Lost opportunities are excluded — 135 matched the window, 6 Closed Lost removed, 129 shown here.
  • Original term: Contract.ContractTerm (months) on the contract each renewal opportunity points back to via Opportunity.ContractId. All 129 opportunities in this window resolved a direct contract link — no account/date inference was needed here, though that fallback is not always available outside this window (org-wide, only ~35% of closed renewals carry a direct contract link).
  • New / quoted term: earliest start to latest end date across recurring line items on the opportunity's primary quote (or most recently modified quote, if none is flagged primary) — 125 of 129 rows. Falls back to the opportunity's own term-length field for 2 rows, and is unresolved for 2 early-stage Discovery opportunities with no dated quote yet (marked unknown).
  • Classification: <24 months = Single-Year, ≥24 months = Multi-Year, applied identically to both the original and new term.
  • Dollar figures use Opportunity.Year_1_ACV__c — the first year's annualized value of the renewal, not total contract value across the new term. The table splits this into its two underlying columns, Renewal_ACV__c (the renewing portion, "Renewal") and Net_New_ACV__c (upsell/expansion added at renewal, "Net New"). The "Total Renewal ACV" figure at the top sums Renewal_ACV__c only, excluding net new.
  • Status: Closed Won opportunities reflect a confirmed outcome; Open Pipeline opportunities reflect the currently quoted term and can still change before close.
  • Account Health is Account.Pulse__c (CX-submitted); Pricing & Packaging is Account.New_Pricing_Packaging_Status__c. Both are read as of today and are not specific to the renewal term.
  • AI Maturity, Degreed.AI Exposure, AI Maturity Last Assessed, and AI Maturity Notes are read from the related Account (Account.AI_Maturity__c, Account.Degreed_AI_Exposure__c, Account.AI_Maturity_Last_Assessed__c, Account.AI_Maturity_Notes__c), not the opportunity itself, and are not specific to the renewal term.
  • Fiscal quarter filter groups rows by the fiscal quarter of their Close Date (FY runs Feb 1–Jan 31, e.g. Aug–Oct 2026 is FQ3 FY27); Owner filters to a single opportunity owner.
  • This is a forward-looking snapshot, not a historical trend — 125 of 129 rows are still open pipeline, so quoted terms can change before these opportunities close.
  • Refresh from Salesforce re-runs the query pipeline through the Salesforce connector under Degreed Internal MCP and recomputes the fiscal window from today's date, so it stays current quarter-over-quarter. It requires that connector to be added under claude.ai Settings → Connectors for whoever is viewing the page.
