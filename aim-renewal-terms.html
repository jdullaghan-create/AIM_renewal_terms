Renewal Opportunities in Salesforce are created 12 months before contract expiration.

Methodology & data notes

  • Population: Opportunities with Record Type Renewals and Close Date between Aug 1, 2026 and Jul 31, 2027 (current fiscal quarter through three quarters out; FY runs Feb 1–Jan 31) — 136 matched the window. Closed Lost opportunities are included and filterable via the Status filter, rather than excluded.
  • Original term: Contract.ContractTerm (months) on the contract each renewal opportunity points back to via Opportunity.ContractId. All 136 opportunities in this window resolved a direct contract link — no account/date inference was needed here, though that fallback is not always available outside this window (org-wide, only ~35% of closed renewals carry a direct contract link).
  • New / quoted term: earliest start to latest end date across recurring line items on the opportunity's primary quote (or most recently modified quote, if none is flagged primary) — 131 of 136 rows. Falls back to the opportunity's own term-length field for 3 rows, and is unresolved for 2 rows with no dated quote yet (marked unknown).
  • Classification: <24 months = Single-Year, ≥24 months = Multi-Year, applied identically to both the original and new term.
  • Dollar figures use Opportunity.Year_1_ACV__c — the first year's annualized value of the renewal, not total contract value across the new term. The table splits this into its two underlying columns, Renewal_ACV__c (the renewing portion, "Renewal") and Net_New_ACV__c (upsell/expansion added at renewal, "Net New"). The "Total Renewal ACV" figure at the top sums Renewal_ACV__c only, excluding net new.
  • Status: Closed Won opportunities reflect a confirmed outcome; Closed Lost opportunities did not renew; Open Pipeline opportunities reflect the currently quoted term and can still change before close.
  • Account Health is Account.Pulse__c (CX-submitted); Pricing & Packaging is Account.New_Pricing_Packaging_Status__c. Both are read as of today and are not specific to the renewal term.
  • HRIS + AI-layer threat scores each renewal's account as Active, Elevated, Watch or Low. Sentences are scanned for Workday, Sana, TechWolf, Eightfold, Gloat and SAP AI (Joule, SAP Talent/Skills/Learning); plain SAP or SuccessFactors counts only beside consolidation language. Inputs: Account.Competitive_Threat_Gong_AI__c and Account.Account_Summary_Gong_AI__c (account.json fills in when Salesforce is blank); displacement_flag and displacement_rival from aim_diagnostic.json; competitive and technology claims from signals.json; HRIS from Account.Primary_HRIS_HCM__c/Secondary_HRIS_HCM__c and the learning system from Account.Primary_LMS_SS__c or account.json.
  • Threat tiers: Active = a vendor named, and a sentence that names a vendor also contains consolidat, terminat, replac, RFP, umbrella, churn, non-renew, cancel or displac. Elevated = a vendor named without that language, or displacement_flag is true with a named rival, or Workday is both the HRIS and the learning system, or one of these HRIS + AI-layer combinations is present: Workday + Sana, Workday + Eightfold, Workday + TechWolf, SAP + SAP Talent Intelligence Hub, SAP + TechWolf, SAP + Eightfold, SAP + Sana, SAP + Gloat, Oracle + Sana. The HRIS side can come from the Salesforce or client-file HRIS field or from the text, and a combination never scores below Elevated. Sentences that only report a vendor's absence do not count. Watch = Workday or SAP SuccessFactors is the HRIS or learning system. Low = none of these. A negated Gong line such as "No competitive mentions", and any sentence that says a vendor was not found or is not a displacement threat, is ignored.
  • Coverage: the AIM client folders in Google Drive (one folder per client, with account.json, signals.json and aim_diagnostic.json) are matched to renewals by Salesforce Account Id. A tier with an asterisk was scored without a complete AIM diagnostic: the folder has no aim_diagnostic.json, or the client has no folder and the score uses Salesforce only. The score is a signal read from text and is not a forecast. It does not use the delta score. The threat data snapshot is dated 2026-10-14.
  • AI Maturity, Degreed.AI Exposure, AI Maturity Last Assessed, and AI Maturity Notes are read from the related Account (Account.AI_Maturity__c, Account.Degreed_AI_Exposure__c, Account.AI_Maturity_Last_Assessed__c, Account.AI_Maturity_Notes__c), not the opportunity itself, and are not specific to the renewal term. A value of 0 on AI Maturity is a completed score, not a blank one.
  • Single/Multi-Year w/AI Scoring tiles show, among renewals of an expiring single-year (or multi-year) contract, what share have any AI Maturity score recorded (0 counts as scored; blank does not) — computed over whatever rows the filters currently select.
  • Owner's manager is Opportunity.Owner.Manager.Name (the renewal owner's manager), collapsed to Carolyn Freeman, Devlin Janssens, Craig Hohls, Patricia Kramer, or Jeff Smith, with every other manager (or no manager) grouped as "Other".
  • Fiscal quarter filter groups rows by the fiscal quarter of their Close Date (FY runs Feb 1–Jan 31, e.g. Aug–Oct 2026 is FQ3 FY27); Owner filters to a single opportunity owner.
  • All filters apply together across the summary cards, chart, and table below — not just the table.
  • This is a forward-looking snapshot, not a historical trend — most rows are still open pipeline, so quoted terms can change before these opportunities close.
  • Refresh from Salesforce + Drive re-runs the query pipeline through the Salesforce connector under Degreed Internal MCP, recomputes the fiscal window from today's date, then reloads the HRIS + AI-layer threat inputs from Salesforce and the AIM client folders in Google Drive (only changed Drive files are re-read). It requires the Salesforce connector (under Degreed Internal MCP) and the Google Drive connector to be added under claude.ai Settings → Connectors for whoever is viewing the page. Without Google Drive, the Salesforce refresh still runs and the threat column keeps its last Drive snapshot.
